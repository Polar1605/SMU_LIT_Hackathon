# The six contracts AITHENA read

Synthetic documents between invented companies — no real client material appears anywhere in
this project. Read them first, then open AITHENA and click any clause reference to see the
same passage highlighted in the source.

1. **Cloud Subscription Agreement** — auto-renews; notice period sits in cl. 12.3, away from the renewal clause
   `01 Cloud Subscription Agreement.pdf` (ingested as `saas-subscription.pdf`)

2. **Exclusive Distribution Agreement (Apex)** — grants exclusive Singapore rights
   `02 Exclusive Distribution Agreement Apex.pdf` (ingested as `distribution-a.pdf`)

3. **Distribution Agreement (Lionbridge)** — grants sole rights over the same territory and product
   `03 Distribution Agreement Lionbridge.pdf` (ingested as `distribution-b.pdf`)

4. **Master Services Agreement (Halcyon)** — liability cap with a possible carve-out in the indemnity schedule
   `04 Master Services Agreement Halcyon.pdf` (ingested as `msa.pdf`)

5. **Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Vantage)** — no exclusivity provision at all; originally a Word file
   `05 Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement Vantage.pdf` (ingested as `mutual-nda.docx`)

6. **Supply Agreement (Foundry)** — scanned, no text layer; notice in business days
   `06 Supply Agreement Foundry.pdf` (ingested as `supplier-agreement-scanned.pdf`)
